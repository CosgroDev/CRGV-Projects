# Welcome

Hi there! Welcome to my corner of the internet. I'm Dale, a 35-year-old lad from Barnsley, and this is where I'll be sharing my thoughts on life, darts, and the various projects I'm working on.

## What to Expect

This blog will be a mix of different things:

- **Darts & Projects**: Updates about my darts team (West Green WMC) and any tech projects I'm building

I've always enjoyed learning and understanding how things work. Whether it's tinkering with cars, building web apps, or diving into AI models, I love the process of figuring things out.

This blog is a way to document that journey and share what I learn along the way.
